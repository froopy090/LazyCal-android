# Privacy Policy for LazyCal

**Effective Date:** May 2, 2026

Francesco Canossi ("I", "me", or "my") built the **LazyCal** app as a free, open-source application. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use "as is". 

My core philosophy with LazyCal is absolute privacy. Because this app utilizes a local AI model, your data stays on your device. This Privacy Policy outlines exactly how the app functions regarding your device permissions and data.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

**LazyCal does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.** You do not need to create an account to use the app, and I do not operate any servers that receive your personal data.

### Camera and Photo Gallery Permissions
To function as a calorie tracker, LazyCal requires access to your device's Camera and Photo Gallery.
* **Why it's needed:** This permission is strictly used to allow you to take pictures of your food or select existing food images from your gallery for the AI to analyze.
* **How your data is handled:** All image processing is done **100% locally** on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to any external server, cloud storage, or third-party database. 

### AI Model Download
Upon first use, LazyCal requires an internet connection to download the necessary AI model (Gemma 4 LiteRT) directly from Hugging Face. 
* This is a standard file download to make the local AI functional. 
* No user data, photos, or inputs are sent to Hugging Face or me during this download or at any point during your use of the AI.

## 2. Analytics, Tracking, and Ads

This app is completely free of advertisements and tracking software.
* **No Analytics or Crash Reporting:** I do not use services like Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. If the app crashes, no crash reports are sent to me. (If you experience bugs, please report them manually via the GitHub repository issues page or via email).
* **No Ad Tracking:** There are no advertisements in the app, meaning no third-party advertising networks are collecting data on your device or behavior.

## 3. Third-Party Links (Donations)

The only external link within the app is a completely optional button that directs users to my Ko-fi donation page. 
* If you choose to click this link, you will be directed to a third-party website (Ko-fi) operated by a different company.
* I have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of Ko-fi or any other third-party sites. I advise you to review their Privacy Policy if you choose to make a donation.

## 4. Children’s Privacy

LazyCal does not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Since the app collects no personal data to begin with, no data from children is ever transmitted or stored.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. I will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
